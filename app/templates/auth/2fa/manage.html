About Two-Factor Authentication

What is 2FA?

Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account by requiring a second form of verification in addition to your password.

Backup Codes

Backup codes are one-time use codes that can be used if you lose access to your authenticator app. Keep them in a secure location.

Device Remembering

You can choose to remember trusted devices for 30 days to avoid entering 2FA codes on devices you use frequently.

Security Tips
  • Keep your backup codes in a secure, offline location
  • Don't share your backup codes with anyone
  • Regenerate backup codes if you think they may have been compromised
  • Enable 2FA on all your important accounts
